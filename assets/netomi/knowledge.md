## Delivery day
- The delivery team texts the phone number on the order the evening before, and calls about 30 minutes before arriving.
- Someone 18 or older needs to be home to let the team in.
- The delivery team installs the appliance in the same visit and takes the old one away at no charge.
- For a dishwasher, clear the cabinet under the sink so the installer can reach the water shut-off valve.

## Changes and cancellations
- Delivery times, installations, and orders are changed or cancelled by a human agent. The support agent transfers the customer.

## Warranty
- Every appliance has the maker's one-year warranty. Installation work is covered by Summit for 90 days.
